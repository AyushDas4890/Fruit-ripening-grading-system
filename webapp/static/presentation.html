01 · the problem

Ripeness is a ladder, not a list

Why grade ripeness?
  • Sorting lines decide sell now, store, or discard
  • Humans disagree near the borders between stages
  • A camera + CNN gives a fast, consistent grade
0unripe
1partially ripe
2ripe
3rotten

True grade rotten (3). Which mistake is worse?

says ripe (2)off by 1
says unripe (0)off by 3

03 · keeping the test honest

Same fruit must never sit in train and test

Wrong ideas

Hash whole crop 8×8 → chained 93 different dark avocados into one group

Single 70/15/15 split → only 2 ripe avocados in test

The best ideas

Group: same photo, burst shots (nearby photo numbers), near-identical crops → 159 groups

5-fold grouped cross-validation: every crop tested once, by a model that never saw it

04 · the method

Ask three yes/no questions

Softmax: 4 unrelated boxes

unripepartially riperiperotten

Cross-entropy only checks “right box or not”.

Ordinal: cumulative targets

0123
grade > 0 ?0111
grade > 1 ?0011
grade > 2 ?0001

Predicted grade = number of “yes” answers.
Being 3 grades off breaks 3 answers, 1 grade off breaks only 1.

04 · the method

The CORAL head

Shared w → answers can never contradict (“>2” is never likelier than “>1”)

loss = Σₖ BCE(σ(s+bₖ), [y>k])

Cao, Mirjalili & Raschka 2020 – “Rank consistent ordinal regression”

05 · models & training

A fair, controlled comparison

3 models

same folds · same seeds · same schedule

Small CNN

4 × [conv-BN-ReLU ×2, max-pool], dropout 0.3. From scratch, softmax.

ResNet18 + softmax

pretrained backbone, linear → 4 logits, cross-entropy

ResNet18 + ordinal

same backbone, CORAL head, 3 thresholds at 10× LR

Freeze → unfreeze

3 epochs head only, then all layers, backbone at 10× lower LR

Optimiser

AdamW 1e-3, cosine schedule, early stop on val MAE, AMP on 4 GB GPU

Augmentation

crop, flips, ±20° rotation, light. Never hue – colour is the label
